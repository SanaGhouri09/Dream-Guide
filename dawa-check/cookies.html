Cookies and storage

Last updated 18 October 2026

Asaan lafzon mein

Zaroori settings (language, install reminder aur aap ki choice) sirf aap ke phone par save hoti hain. Google Analytics cookies optional hain. Europe ke visitors se pehle poocha jata hai, baqi sab neeche wale button se isay band kar sakte hain.

Essential items never leave your device and can't identify you. The two Google Analytics cookies are only set when analytics is on.

Who gets asked first

If your device's time zone is in Europe, analytics stays off and you'll see a short banner asking first. Everywhere else, including Pakistan, analytics is on by default and you can turn it off with the button above. Turning it off deletes the Google Analytics cookies straight away.

Advertising

Dawa Check shows no ads today. If that changes, this page will list the ad cookies before they're used, and they'll stay off until you agree.

Clearing everything

You can delete all of the above from your browser's settings, under site data or cookies. The tool keeps working; it just forgets your language.
