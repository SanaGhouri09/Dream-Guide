Questions

How many fake medicines has DRAP found in Pakistan?

DawaCheck's list holds 756 DRAP recall and rapid alerts from Jul 2021 to 29 Jul 2026: 248 for fake (spurious) batches and 417 for batches that failed lab tests. Every alert links to DRAP's own notice.

How do I check if my medicine batch is fake?

Find the batch number on the box or strip (often marked B. No. or Lot), then type it into the batch check on dawacheck.pk. It matches the number against every DRAP alert here. A batch that isn't listed hasn't been flagged by DRAP, which isn't the same as proof it's genuine.

What should I do if my medicine is on this list?

Stop using it, keep the box and receipt, and report it to DRAP on 0800-03727 or through the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. Talk to your doctor about a replacement.
