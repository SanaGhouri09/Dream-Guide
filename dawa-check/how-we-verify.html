Data and method

How we verify

Last updated 18 October 2026

Asaan lafzon mein

Har qeemat aur har alert DRAP ke public documents se liya gaya hai. Har alert ke saath DRAP ka asal link hai, taake aap khud check kar sakein. Batch number mein space ya dash se farq nahi parta. Koi ghalti nazar aaye to footer se batayein, hum DRAP source se mila kar theek kar dete hain.

Two sources, both from DRAP

Prices

Prices come from DRAP's published price list for registered medicines: 21,534 packs at the last update. For each pack we keep the brand name, the composition, the company, the pack size, the price in rupees, the date that price took effect and the DRAP registration number. The site shows the effective date and registration number with every price, so you can match it to the notification.

Alerts

Alerts come from DRAP's product recall and rapid alert notices on dra.gov.pk, from July 2021 to 29 July 2026: 756 entries. For each one we keep the product, the batch number, the company, the reason, the date and a link to the original notice. We sort each alert into one of four types using DRAP's own wording: fake (spurious), failed lab test (substandard), unregistered, or recall.

How the data gets onto the site

  1. We collect the latest price list and alert notices from DRAP's website.
  2. We copy each field as DRAP prints it. Prices aren't rounded or adjusted.
  3. When one notice lists several batch numbers, we split them so each batch can be matched on its own.
  4. If the price list has the same pack twice with different start dates, we keep only the newest price. Packs listed with no price (Rs 0) are left out.
  5. We keep the link to the DRAP notice with every alert, so anyone can check our work in one tap.
  6. The date in the footer is the last time the data changed.

How matching works

Batch check

Batch numbers are printed in many styles, so we compare them in capital letters and ignore spaces, dashes, dots and slashes. "139-n 086" and "139N086" count as the same batch.

  • Red, DRAP ALERT: your batch exactly matches a batch in a DRAP alert.
  • Amber, check again: it's a close match. At least five characters line up, so re-read the box carefully.
  • No alert: DRAP hasn't issued an alert for that batch in the period we cover.

Price check

You can search by any part of a brand name. Results are grouped by brand and company, then you pick the strength and pack size. We compare what you paid with DRAP's price and ignore differences under Rs 0.50, which usually come from rounding at the till.

What we can't tell you

  • Whether a batch with no alert is genuine. We don't test medicines; we only report DRAP's findings.
  • Whether DRAP issued a new price or alert after the date in the footer.
  • The legal price of a medicine that isn't in DRAP's list. For those, the price printed on the box is the limit.

Corrections

Found a wrong price, a missing alert or a typo? Send the medicine name, pack size and a screenshot to hello@dawacheck.pk. We compare it with the DRAP source and fix it in the next update.

Independence

Dawa Check takes no money from drug companies, distributors or pharmacies. If we ever accept a sponsor, it'll be named on the page, and it will never change a price, an alert or the order of results.
