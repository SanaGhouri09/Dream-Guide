Questions

What is the price of Artecid in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for Artecid Tablets (8’s) is Rs 543.79, in effect since 20 Aug, 2026. Across all 2 Artecid packs on the DRAP list, prices run from Rs 543.79 to Rs 638.33. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 18 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Artecid?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.

How do I know if my Artecid is fake?

DRAP has issued 1 alert that name Artecid; they're listed on this page with the batch numbers. Compare the batch number printed on your box, or type it into the batch check on dawacheck.pk.
