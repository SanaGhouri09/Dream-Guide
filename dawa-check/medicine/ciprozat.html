Questions

What is the price of Ciprozat in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for Ciprozat 250 mg tablets (1 x10) is Rs 247.10, in effect since 04 Nov, 2025. Across all 2 Ciprozat packs on the DRAP list, prices run from Rs 247.10 to Rs 494.21. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 20 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Ciprozat?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.

How do I know if my Ciprozat is fake?

DRAP has issued 1 alert that name Ciprozat; they're listed on this page with the batch numbers. Compare the batch number printed on your box, or type it into the batch check on dawacheck.pk.
