Questions

What is the price of Coam in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for Coam 1.2gm Injection (1's) is Rs 170.00, in effect since 14 Sep, 2026. Across all 6 Coam packs on the DRAP list, prices run from Rs 149.00 to Rs 312.00. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 20 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Coam?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.

How do I know if my Coam is fake?

None of the 756 DRAP fake and substandard alerts in our list names Coam. That doesn't prove any one box is genuine, so check the batch number printed on your box with the batch check on dawacheck.pk.
