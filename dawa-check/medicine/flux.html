Questions

What is the price of Flux in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for Flux Capsules 20mg (7's) is Rs 514.54, in effect since 27 Jul, 2026. Across all 5 Flux packs on the DRAP list, prices run from Rs 64.98 to Rs 974.54. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 20 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Flux?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.

How do I know if my Flux is fake?
