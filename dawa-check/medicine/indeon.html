Questions

What is the price of Indeon in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for Indeon Injection 8mg/4ml (4MLX1'S) is Rs 784.96, in effect since 23 Jul, 2026. Across all 3 Indeon packs on the DRAP list, prices run from Rs 784.96 to Rs 3,924.78. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 20 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Indeon?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.

How do I know if my Indeon is fake?
