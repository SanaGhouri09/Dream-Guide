Questions

What is the price of Lopra in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for Lopra Capsule 2mg (6x10's) is Rs 294.14, in effect since 13 May, 2026. Across all 2 Lopra packs on the DRAP list, prices run from Rs 294.14 to Rs 490.23. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 18 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Lopra?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.

How do I know if my Lopra is fake?
