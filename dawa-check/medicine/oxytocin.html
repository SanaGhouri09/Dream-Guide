Questions

What is the price of Oxytocin in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for Oxytocin 5IU/ml Injection (50's ampoules) is Rs 759.41, in effect since 01 Jul, 2025. Across all 4 Oxytocin packs on the DRAP list, prices run from Rs 146.51 to Rs 759.41. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 20 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Oxytocin?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.

How do I know if my Oxytocin is fake?

DRAP has issued 5 alerts that name Oxytocin; they're listed on this page with the batch numbers. Compare the batch number printed on your box, or type it into the batch check on dawacheck.pk.
