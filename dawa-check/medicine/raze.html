Questions

What is the price of Raze in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for Raze 4mg Tablet (10's) is Rs 626.23, in effect since 28 Sep, 2026. Across all 4 Raze packs on the DRAP list, prices run from Rs 254.83 to Rs 637.07. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 20 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Raze?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.

How do I know if my Raze is fake?
