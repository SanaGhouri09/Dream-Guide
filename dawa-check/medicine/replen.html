Questions

What is the price of Replen in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for REPLEN Sachet (1s) is Rs 18.50, in effect since 25 Aug, 2025. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 18 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Replen?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.
