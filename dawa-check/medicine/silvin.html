What is the price of Silvin in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for Silvin Cream 1% w/w (15 gm) is Rs 97.20, in effect since 20 Aug, 2026. Across all 3 Silvin packs on the DRAP list, prices run from Rs 97.20 to Rs 546.11. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 20 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Silvin?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.
