Questions

What is the price of Viron in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for Viron Capsule (10's) is Rs 320.13, in effect since 23 Jul, 2026. Across all 3 Viron packs on the DRAP list, prices run from Rs 320.13 to Rs 888.08. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 20 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Viron?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.

How do I know if my Viron is fake?
