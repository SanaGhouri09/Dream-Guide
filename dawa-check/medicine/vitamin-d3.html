Questions

What is the price of Vitamin D3 in Pakistan?

DRAP's official price for Vitamin D3 5mg/ml Injection Oral/IM (1's) is Rs 110.00, in effect since 04 Aug, 2026. These are DRAP's maximum retail prices, last checked against the DRAP list on 18 Oct 2026.

What if the pharmacy charges more than this for Vitamin D3?

Show the pharmacist the DRAP price first. If they won't correct it, call the DRAP helpline on 0800-03727 or file a complaint with DRAP or the Pakistan Citizen's Portal. The price check on dawacheck.pk works out how much extra you paid and drafts the complaint for you.
