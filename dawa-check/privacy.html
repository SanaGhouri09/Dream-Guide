Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Asaan lafzon mein

Hum aap ka naam, phone number, CNIC ya prescription nahi maangte. Dawa ki search aur jo qeemat aap likhte hain, woh aap ke phone par hi check hoti hai. Analytics on ho to sirf yeh count hota hai ke kaun se pages aur dawaein dekhi gayin. Footer mein Cookie settings se isay kabhi bhi band kar sakte hain.

Who we are

Dawa Check is a free website that shows the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan's (DRAP) official medicine prices and drug safety alerts. In this policy, "we" means the people who run Dawa Check. You can reach us at hello@dawacheck.pk.

What we never ask for

There's no account and no login. We don't ask for your name, phone number, CNIC, address, prescriptions or payment details, and there's no form on the site that collects them.

What happens to your searches

The medicine name you type, the batch number you check and the amount you say you paid are all matched inside your browser, against data that was downloaded with the page. They aren't sent to our server and we can't see them, unless analytics is switched on (see below).

The complaint text the site drafts for you is also made on your phone. It only goes anywhere if you copy it and send it yourself.

What's stored on your device

A few small settings are saved in your browser so the site remembers you chose English, or that you closed the "add to home screen" bar. The full list is on the Cookies page. Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

Analytics, only when it's on

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used. For visitors whose device is set to a European time zone it stays off until they agree. Everyone else can switch it off at any time from .

When it's on, Google Analytics records:

  • pages viewed, the website or app that sent you, and your device and browser type;
  • your approximate city or region, which Google works out from your IP address (Google Analytics 4 doesn't store the IP address itself);
  • which medicine names were searched, whether a checked batch matched a DRAP alert, and whether a price you entered was above the legal price and by roughly how much;
  • taps on the DRAP helpline, WhatsApp and "copy complaint" buttons.

We use this to see which medicines people check most, where overcharging shows up, and what in the site is confusing. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't combine it with any other data about you. Analytics data is kept for 14 months. Google's handling of it is covered by Google's privacy policy, and Google offers a browser add-on that blocks Google Analytics on every site.

Other services that see your visit

  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested) to keep the site running and stop abuse.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees a request from your browser when the page opens.
  • Links to DRAP, WhatsApp, X and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

When you contact us

If you email us to report a mistake, we see whatever you send. We use it only to check and fix the data, and we don't add you to any list.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it with pharmacies or drug companies. We may publish totals, such as "the 10 medicines people checked most this month". Those numbers never identify a person.

Children

Dawa Check is meant for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live. If we ever add advertising, ad cookies will stay off until you agree to them.
