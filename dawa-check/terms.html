3. Accuracy

We copy prices and alerts from DRAP's public documents and link to the source wherever we can. Even so, DRAP can issue a new price or alert after our last update, and a copying mistake is always possible. If our data and an official DRAP notification disagree, the DRAP notification is correct. A batch with no alert on Dawa Check hasn't been tested by us and isn't guaranteed to be genuine.
